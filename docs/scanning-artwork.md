## Prompt set

Shared direction: Create a NEW standalone wide landscape illustration for an Infinite Variance (IV Outreach) dark desktop app. The attached screenshot is style reference ONLY: don't draw UI, cards, text, or copy a composition. Match the vintage copperplate engraving / screen-printed editorial style: precise dense curved hatch lines, patterned linework, warm aged ivory highlights, rich true crimson red and blackened wine ink on a near-black charcoal background. Use red rather than pink. Striking at a small size, with one clear visual subject, real etched texture, and a little atmospheric grain. Matte, refined, mysterious, high contrast in the artwork, no glossy 3D. No letters, words, numbers, icons with text, captions, logos, borders, framing, or watermark. Full bleed.

- **Radar observatory:** Panoramic Scanning page banner. An engraved observatory horizon with an abstract all-seeing eye formed from concentric radar waves at far right, opposing fine ribbons sweeping in from left and a sparse constellation of precise signal points. Center is mostly near black and low detail to support large centered white type. Make the motifs legible in a shallow horizontal center crop. Aim 2.5:1 or widest landscape.
- **Magnifying lens:** Opportunity scan artwork: a large vintage magnifying lens over layered website-like geometric sheets, revealing a tiny crimson star and an engraved eye reflected in the glass. Hero cluster sits in the middle-right, quiet near-black left half. Wide landscape.
- **Town map and pin:** Local markets artwork: an overhead engraved town map with curved streets and a small cluster of ivory buildings and one vivid scarlet map pin, like an atlas engraving. Entire recognizable cluster in the middle-right, quiet near-black left half. Wide landscape.
- **Eye and research network:** Agent research artwork: an engraved eye woven into a constellation of branches and small linked research nodes, with two subtle nested profiles in negative space. A neural-research impression, distinct from magnifying glass or map. Main motif centered vertically on the right half; quiet near-black left half. Wide landscape.
- **Compass:** Custom search artwork: a vintage directional compass with a strong crimson needle and engraved crosshairs, over one curved trail through nearly black negative space, no compass letters. Main motif centered vertically on the right half; quiet near-black left half. Wide landscape.
- **Cut ruby:** Premium discovery artwork: a refined cut ruby crystal opened by layered etched ribbons and small ivory discovery sparks, as an elevated precision instrument. No coin, crown, lock, or map. Main motif centered vertically on the right half; quiet near-black left half. Wide landscape.
- **Bell and broadsheet:** Watchlists and news artwork: a narrow field of engraved signal waves with a sculptural ivory bell and folded blank broadsheet, crimson alert arc behind. No readable newspaper text. Main motif centered vertically on the right half; quiet near-black left half. Wide landscape.
- **Quill and folio:** Content briefs artwork: an engraved folded creative folio with a quill tracing a scarlet ribbon across two blank ivory pages and a few ink stipples. The notion of writing and collaboration, not an ID card. Main motif centered vertically on the right half; quiet near-black left half. Wide landscape.
